2. What Information We Collect

We collect and process only the minimal information strictly required to deliver our AI career optimization services:

  • Account Data: Full name, email address, avatar photo (if uploaded or authenticated via Google OAuth), and secure authentication user IDs managed through Supabase Auth.
  • Resume & Career Inputs: Work history, skills, education, target job descriptions, uploaded PDF resume text, and customized output documents.
  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Data: If you supply your personal LLM API keys (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic), they are stored strictly locally in your browser's encrypted local storage. We never log, store, or inspect your personal API keys on our servers.
  • Technical & Session Analytics: Browser type, timestamps, ATS score metrics, and feature usage counters (e.g. tracking your 5 Free tier generations).

3. How We Use Artificial Intelligence (AI)

To perform contextual tailoring, keyword matching, and executive formatting, your input data is sent to enterprise Large Language Model (LLM) providers (Google Gemini API, OpenAI API, Anthropic Claude API).

Enterprise Privacy Standards: We interact with AI providers through commercial API agreements where data transmission is encrypted (TLS 1.3) and your career data is never retained to train future AI models.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Art. 6)

We process your personal data under the following lawful grounds:

  • Performance of Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): To operate the platform, tailor your CV against job descriptions, and store your generation history.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): To safeguard infrastructure security, prevent fraudulent abuse of free credits, and maintain platform stability.
  • User Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For voluntary communications and support interactions.

5. Data Storage, Retention & Security

All user records, profiles, and generation histories are securely housed in PostgreSQL databases provided by Supabase with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring that only you have access to your data.

Retention Period: Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. You can delete past generations individually or request full account erasure at any time.

6. Your Data Rights

Under GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection standards, you have the following guaranteed rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request immediate permanent deletion of your account and all associated resumes.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your CV history and Markdown data in open, standard formats.
  • Right to Object / Restrict Processing: Request restrictions on specific processing activities.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at vineetsansare@gmail.com. We fulfill all verified requests within 30 days.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use advertising cookies. We use only strictly necessary first-party cookies and localStorage tokens for authentication sessions, dark/light theme preferences, and locally saved BYOK API key configurations.
